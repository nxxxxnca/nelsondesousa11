I was born in 2009 in Venezuela, in the state of Guárico, in Altagracia de Orituco. I spent the first five years of my life in Guaribe, a small town in the Venezuelan plains.

I consider myself a calm and charismatic person. I've always enjoyed football, a sport I play at Villa Sport Aragua. I also like programming and software development, and I've been interested in video games since I was little.
